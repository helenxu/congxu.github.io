Research Interests

  • Statistical machine learning: computational statistics, combination of neural networks and traditional statistical models, data analysis in complex systems.
  • Statistical methods for biological and medical research: survival analysis, longitudinal data analysis, adaptive clinical trial design.

Positions

  • Lecturer ⟶ Teaching Associate Professor  Dec 2019 – Present

    Responsibilities: teaching, research, and public service.
  • Algorithm Researcher  Oct 2017 – Dec 2019
    Cloud and Smart Industry Group, Tencent Technology, Shenzhen, China
    Implementation of state-of-the-art algorithms into recommendation systems; application of natural language processing and knowledge graph to projects.
  • Postdoctoral Researcher  Oct 2015 – Oct 2017
    Plevritis Lab, School of Medicine, Stanford University, Stanford, California, USA
    Stochastic simulation modeling of the impact of cancer screening and treatment strategies on cancer incidence and mortality. Advisor: Sylvia Plevritis.
  • Senior Biostatistician  Jul 2014 – Sep 2015
    Integrated Quantitative Sciences, Novartis Pharmaceuticals, Shanghai, China
    Statistical analysis of randomized controlled clinical trials.
