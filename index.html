About Me

I am a Teaching Associate Professor in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), Shenzhen, China. Before joining SUSTech in 2019, I was an algorithm researcher at Tencent, a postdoctoral researcher in the Plevritis Lab at Stanford University School of Medicine, and a senior biostatistician at Novartis Pharmaceuticals. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of California, Davis (2014), and my B.S. in Statistics from the University of Science and Technology of China (2009).

Research Interests

  • Biomedical Statistics: survival analysis, longitudinal data analysis.
  • Adaptive Design of Clinical Trials: sequential monitoring, response-adaptive randomization.
  • Analysis of Complex Networks: statistical modeling and data analysis of complex systems.
