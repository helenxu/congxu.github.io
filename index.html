I am a Teaching Associate Professor of Statistics at the Southern University of Science and Technology. I received my PhD in Statistics from University of California, Davis in 2014, where I specialized in joint modeling of survival and longitudinal data.

My research focuses on survival analysis, adaptive design of clinical trials, and analysis of complex networks. I have published in the Journal of Statistical Software, Biostatistics, Nature Medicine, Nature Communications, and PNAS.
